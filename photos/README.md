Put your newsletter photos in this folder, named `photo-01.jpg` through `photo-09.jpg`:

- photo-01 — wide cover photo at the top
- photo-02 — feature story photo
- photo-03 — highlights photo
- photo-04 … photo-09 — gallery grid

Using different names or `.png`/`.jpeg` files? Update both the `src` and the download `href` for that photo in `index.html`.
